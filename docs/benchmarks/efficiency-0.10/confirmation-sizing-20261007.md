# Confirmation planning after the negative screening

The frozen T0–T2 candidate has total-cost D/A 1.5427; all six screened families
have ratios greater than one. G3 requires at most 0.8 and no increase in uncached
input. A larger sample cannot make the observed candidate satisfy that threshold.
G5 is **not scheduled**: first establish a useful signal with a separately
identified candidate. The completed authorization was 24 turns; all 24 were used,
although 493,940 uncached-input tokens remain below its aggregate ceiling.
That remaining token allowance does not authorize more turns.

The sample standard deviation of paired log cost ratios is 0.3689. For planning
only, if a future candidate's true ratio were 0.65, the normal approximation for
80% power to place a one-sided 95% upper bound below 0.8 is
`ceil(((1.645 + 0.842) * 0.3689 / log(0.8 / 0.65))²) = 20` independent tasks.
Balancing six families suggests at least 24 independent held-out tasks. Two
replicas per condition would require 96 solver turns; replicas do not increase
the independent task count. At this screening's 21,085.8 mean uncached input per
turn, that is approximately 2,024,240 uncached input, before contingencies.
This is a conditional planning calculation, **not an authorized campaign**, an
assumption of future improvement or a verified monetary estimate. Related
screening templates and repository heterogeneity make the variance provisional.

Such a sample would still not establish general quality noninferiority within
5 percentage points. For perspective, even zero critical failures in 59
independent candidate tasks yields a one-sided 95% binomial upper failure bound
of about 5%; that is an absolute failure bound, not a paired noninferiority test.
A confirmatory proposal must preregister its paired quality margin, independent
repository/task sampling, critical rename/signature criterion and suitable power
or confidence procedure. Controlled static source oracles can support a narrowly
stated source-edit conclusion; they do not certify runtime behavior or coverage
of languages/clients/models not actually tested.

Before any further AI turn, freeze the revised source/binary/provider/client,
actual offered surfaces, independent holdout, oracles, sampling unit, replication,
stop rules and new explicit count/token/economic budget where available. No
preparation or judge AI and no external retries are implied. Keep these costs
separate from the historical smokes and completed screening.
